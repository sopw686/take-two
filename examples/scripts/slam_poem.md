## The drawer [1:15]
My mother kept her voice / in the kitchen drawer, / next to the batteries / and the rubber bands.
She took it out for *weddings* / and for funerals, / and once / for a man at the bank / who called her *sweetheart*.
The rest of the time / she spoke in dishes: / the clatter that meant sit down, / the quiet that meant not now.
I learned to read the plates / before I learned to read.
I learned that silence has a sound / if you set the table right. //
But last night / I opened the drawer.
[KEY] It still works. // It was never broken. / It was only *waiting* / for someone to say it out loud. //
